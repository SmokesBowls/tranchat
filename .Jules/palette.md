## 2024-04-08 - Icon-Only Button Keyboard Focus Ring Patterns
**Learning:** When adding focus states to icon-only buttons (`focus-visible:ring-2`), standard app backgrounds like in `SetPassword.jsx`/`JoinRoom.jsx` should use `focus-visible:ring-blue-500` to match the application's primary color palette. However, when icon buttons are placed on dark or black semi-transparent overlays (like the video chat overlay in `Room.jsx`), the blue ring provides insufficient contrast; `focus-visible:ring-white` must be used instead.
**Action:** When auditing or implementing icon-only buttons, explicitly check the background context and apply `focus-visible:ring-white` for dark overlays and `focus-visible:ring-blue-500` for standard light views. Also ensure the button has `aria-label` and `title` for screen readers and tooltips, and the inner SVG has `aria-hidden="true"`.
