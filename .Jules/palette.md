## 2024-04-25 - Focus Rings on Dark Overlays
**Learning:** Standard focus rings (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-blue-500`) may lack contrast or clash visually when applied to elements sitting on dark, semi-transparent overlays (like the video chat exit button).
**Action:** Use high-contrast focus rings, such as `focus-visible:ring-white`, for interactive elements on dark backgrounds or overlays to ensure visibility during keyboard navigation.
