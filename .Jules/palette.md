## 2024-05-24 - Icon-only buttons accessibility and focus states
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like back buttons or exit buttons) across the app components lack necessary accessibility attributes. They require `aria-label`s for screen readers, `title` tooltips for hover context, and `aria-hidden="true"` on their inner `<svg>` elements to hide decorative icons from screen readers. Furthermore, explicit `focus-visible` styles with appropriate ring colors (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-blue-500` for light backgrounds and `focus-visible:ring-white` for dark overlays) are essential for keyboard navigation visibility.
**Action:** Always verify that icon-only buttons include `aria-label`, `title`, explicit `focus-visible` styling contextual to their background, and `aria-hidden="true"` on inner SVGs.
